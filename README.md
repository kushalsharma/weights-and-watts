# Weights & Watts

Code, prompts and measured numbers from [Weights & Watts](https://www.youtube.com/@WeightsWatts), the YouTube channel that runs new open-weight models on hardware you might own, then on rented cards, and measures everything. Each episode has a folder here, so you can rerun its tests on your own card and hold your numbers up against ours.

| Episode | Folder | Models |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`ep01-h3-image/`](ep01-h3-image/) | MiniMax H3-Image and Pruna's P-H3-Image, on an RTX 3060 12 GB and on Colab's L4 and A100 |

Each folder has its own README, which covers everything from installing to reading the results.

This repository holds code, prompts, settings and numbers. It holds no model weights and no generated pictures: the scripts fetch the weights from the makers' own pages, and those come under the makers' own licences.
